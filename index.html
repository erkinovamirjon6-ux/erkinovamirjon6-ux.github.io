English summary

TOPIK PLUS is a Korean language school in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. “TOPIK PLUS bot” is our internal automation app: a Telegram bot that answers course questions and books free trial lessons, and a YouTube integration that, with our own Google account's permission, uploads lesson videos to our own channel (@TOPIKplus), adds them to playlists, posts quiz comments and reads our channel analytics for weekly reports. It is used only by the TOPIK PLUS team for our own channel.
